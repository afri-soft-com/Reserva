## RESERVA Pro (com.reserva.pro) — 2.1(b) et 1.5

Hello,

Thank you for your review. Please find the information requested below.

**Guideline 2.1(b) – Business model**

RESERVA Pro is the companion app for businesses listed on RESERVA in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (clinics, hotels, restaurants, transport companies, schools, administrative offices). It lets them manage their schedule and the bookings made by their customers in the RESERVA app.

1. *Who are the users of the paid features?* There are no paid features in RESERVA Pro. Its users are business owners and their staff, who use it for free to manage their bookings.
2. *Where can users purchase features and services?* Nothing is sold in RESERVA Pro. The only payments related to the app are made by **customers** in the RESERVA app, by Mobile Money, for real-world services consumed outside the app (a medical appointment, a hotel night, a restaurant table, a bus ticket). Under guideline 3.1.3(e), these physical goods and services are not paid through In-App Purchase. RESERVA Pro only shows providers the bookings they received and the amounts they earn for these services, and lets them request a payout to their Mobile Money account.
3. *What previously purchased features or services can be accessed?* None. No digital content, subscription or feature is purchased outside the app and then unlocked in RESERVA Pro.
4. *What paid content is unlocked without In-App Purchase?* None. All RESERVA Pro features are available to every approved provider at no cost.

**Guideline 1.5** – The Support URL now points to https://reserva-web-b9au.onrender.com/support, a public page explaining how to contact our support team (support@reserva.cd), what information to include, and answers to common questions.

Thank you,
